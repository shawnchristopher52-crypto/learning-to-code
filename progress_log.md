# 📓 Progress Log

> A record of your journey, week by week. Update this every time you finish a week.

This isn't busywork — keeping a log helps in three ways:

1. **You see your growth.** What feels impossible in Week 4 is trivial in Week 14. The log proves it.
2. **You spot patterns.** If you keep getting stuck on the same kind of thing, the log reveals it.
3. **You build a portfolio asset.** Future-you (or a future employer) reading this log sees a thoughtful, self-aware learner.

---

## How to Use This File

After you finish each week, fill in the template below. Be honest. The "what was hard" section is the most useful part long-term — don't skip it.

---

## Template (Copy this for each week)

```
## Week N — Topic — Finished YYYY-MM-DD

### What I built
- (project name + a one-line description)

### What I learned
- (concept 1)
- (concept 2)
- ...

### What was hard
- (something that confused you, even if you eventually figured it out)
- (a bug you hit and how you solved it)

### What I'm proud of
- (one thing — even small)

### Questions still open
- (anything you want to come back to)
```
